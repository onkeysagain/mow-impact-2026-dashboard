What drives us

Our mission

To provide high-quality, accessible Montessori education to children in underserved communities by bringing the classroom to them, fostering independence, creativity, and a love for learning in every child, regardless of location or circumstance.

What rides with us

Rungano is Shona for “telling our stories”: families are children’s first teachers.

  • Respect for the ChildChildren are capable, curious, and worthy of trust.
  • Families, Community & AncestorsFamilies are children’s first educators.
  • Sensory-Based LearningMovement and hands-on exploration fuel learning.
  • Equity & LiberationEducation must center justice and freedom for children of color.
  • JoyousnessChildren’s joy and creativity are the heartbeat of the work.

How change happens

School programs logic model · from exploration to sustainable growth

  1. InputsTeachers, culturally relevant curriculum, Montessori materials, the bus, mentors and community partners.
  2. ActivitiesMontessori programs personalized for each school and student, family outreach and volunteering.
  3. OutputsBus visits, school partnerships, children showing up and building portfolios of their work.
  4. OutcomesChildren engage in culturally relevant learning and can name their passions and areas for growth.
  5. ImpactChildren of color express their joy, creativity, and love of learning.

Ride with us

Every family deserves access to joyful, culturally affirming Montessori learning. Help keep the bus rolling into the next neighborhood, and the next community.
